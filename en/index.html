Scotland: Travel Itineraries & Our Home Away From Home

We discovered the beauty of Scotland more than ten years ago. It took just one trip to know we had found our second home. Since then, we’ve traveled the country from top to bottom, visited iconic landmarks and hidden gems, and eventually, we even got married here. Scotland isn't just a spot on a map for us, it’s where we feel most alive.

We’ve put our experiences together to help you plan your own adventure without the headache. You’ll find practical tips, itineraries that skip the tourist traps, and the kind of raw stories you won't find in guidebooks.

USA & Alaska: Road Trips Through the Wildest National Parks

Thanks to family in the States, we first crossed Atlantic years ago. Until then, we mostly stuck to Europe, but our first USA road trip completely hooked us. We realized how small the world actually is and fell in love with the freedom of life on the road. We’ve since clocked thousands of miles through Arizona, Utah, Nevada, California, and Washington.

Alaska is a whole different beast, raw, rugged, and unforgiving. We’ll show you how to pull off a wilderness trip on your own and what to actually avoid if you don't want to get eaten by a bear.

Practical Tips: How to Plan Your Adventure Without Breaking the Bank

Travelling isn’t just about the views, it’s about surviving the trip and keeping your sanity. Over the years, we’ve figured out what’s a "must-pack" and what’s just junk that’ll drive you crazy on the road. We’ve spent days looking for a place to sleep in the middle of nowhere, and packing for cold so deep it numbs your brain.

You’ll find tips on gear that actually lasts and ways to plan a trip without blowing all your savings. Let’s be real: We’re not millionaires. We’re just here to show you that with a bit of researching, you can go almost anywhere. So, stop overthinking it and come join us!
